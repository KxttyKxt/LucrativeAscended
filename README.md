# Lucrative _Ascended_

<sup><em>When I began working on the first Lucrative, I had a specific image in mind. I would say that it achieved this image, but it was poorly strung and polished only to a fault. Now, Lucrative is soaring to new heights in 1.21!</em></sup>

***

### Up your arsenal 🔥

⚔️ 15 new melee weapon types!  
✨ An in-depth magic system with many utility and combat uses!  
💎 A jewelry system to tailor your gear to suit your needs!

### Conquer the lands 👑

🍻 The full Dungeons 'n' Taverns series!  
🏕️ Extensive structures, both friendly and dangerous!  
🗺️ Put your cartography skills to the test with a new compass waypoint system!  
🎒 Take your inventory management to the next level with bundles and backpacks!

### The ultimate quest 📜

🔮 Explore every corner of the world to gather the Eyes of Ender \[End Remastered\].  
💛 Find, craft, and eat the 48 fabled Golden Foods to permanently increase vitality.  
💢 Some enemies are significantly stronger than others, but the biggest targets always have the best loot.  
☠️ Your achievements will not go unnoticed, however…forces of evil are watching, planning the perfect moment to attack…

### Many delights 🍳

🥪 Experience culinary perfection with Farmer's Delight and other features.  
🍃 Enjoy a world teeming with life in an immersive atmosphere.  
💰 Villagers are aplenty, and most of them thrive. Should you be in need of food, tools, or emeralds, you'll find what you need around every corner.  
📝 Plenty of custom data, integrations, and features await; you won't find them anywhere else!
